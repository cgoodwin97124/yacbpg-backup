## Clarification questions & answers

### 8a. What is the single most annoying thing about the app today? (Not a feature request — the thing that makes you sigh. This decides the order of the UI work.)

- **Answer:** The fact that generation work stops when the browser tab loses focus.  If there's no way around this then we'll live with it, but I really hope there's a way around it. This makes me sigh so, so much.  I really hope there's a way around it.

### 8b. Is there anything you have wanted but never asked for? (Half-formed ideas welcome — they often expose a missing piece of the model.)

- **Answer:** Maybe character dialog for panels?  It doesn't have to render the dialog into the images -- in fact I'd almost prefer that it not.  

I'll probably think of more as I go along.  Really, this has grown up from half-formed ideas stuck together!

### 8c. How should I report progress on a long refactor?

- **Answer:** One short message per release, and a longer summary at the end of each phase (recommended)

### 8d. Do you want a phase-by-phase refactor log kept in the repo (like DEV-NOTES, but for the refactor: what moved where, what was deleted, what gotchas turned up)?

- **Answer:** Yes — a REFACTOR-NOTES.md alongside the other docs (recommended)
- **Note:** To a large extent, I really care most that it works and that I can easily learn and understand how it works.  The DEV-NOTES and AI-NOTES were really there for me to provide you, the AI worker, with continuity and memory that Perchance (and I suppose to an extent AI in general) doesn't have.  At the very beginning of the project I found myself, when something didn't work, explaining the feature from scratch, and I was often doing that on my phone.  I realized pretty early on that you always read the index.html file, so I could put directives and "read me first!" and memory/continuity aids in here.

### 8e. Roughly how often do you use the app, and how much time do you usually have to answer questions like these?

- **Answer:** Most days — and I can answer a batch of questions quickly
- **Note:** The answer form makes it a lot easier for me to answer batches of questions, so let's keep doing this.
